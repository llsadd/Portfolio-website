[Heading1]Visual UniVRs
A VR/AR studio with Olympics Committee–level client work, undersold by a website that explained none of it.
ROLE
End-to-end website design, brand identity, developer management
SCOPE
Full WordPress rebuild, brand identity, pitch deck, social & presentation assets
TOOLS
Figma, WordPress
YEAR
2023–24
RESULT
~2× average project value
[Heading2]The Problem
Visual UniVRs builds VR/AR experiences and graphics good enough to land the Olympics Committee as a client reference — but their website did nothing to earn that kind of trust from a new visitor. Technical, niche services, presented with no real structure or proof.
I started with a two-hour briefing covering target audience, key propositions, services, and the technology itself — dense enough that most of the value came from getting these details right before any design work began.
[Heading2]Research & Decisions
Keyword & competitor research  —  Mapped realistic-to-rank keywords against the ones most likely to bring leads, then studied 5–10 top competitors&apos; structure, content and design — not to copy, but to see what had already been tested.
Findings, documented  —  Packaged the research into a takeaways document, so the client left with marketing and growth insight beyond just a new website.
Prototype before pixels  —  Built structure and content flow first, prioritizing clear navigation so a technical service stayed easy to follow.
Style options, then commitment  —  Presented a few style/layout directions before building the rest of the site, so the final design matched the brand and vision the client actually wanted.
[Heading2]Build
Designed the remaining pages in the chosen direction, then adapted everything for tablet and mobile. I hired and managed a WordPress developer to build it — including replacing an underperforming developer partway through to keep the project on track. After launch, we added a blog with the client&apos;s SEO specialist, plus social covers, a presentation, and a Calendly booking page design.
[Heading2]Outcome
~2×   Average project value, post-launch
6 months   Of team-wide work from new clients found via the site
Client-reported, based on projects won after launch — no paid ads running at the time.
[Heading2]What I&apos;d Do Differently
The developer swap cost real time I hadn&apos;t planned for. Next time, I&apos;d run a small paid trial task before committing a multi-month build to a new developer, instead of discovering the fit problem after work was already underway.
[Heading2]Client Testimonial
“We&apos;re already seeing results — even without running ads yet. The website secured us several large projects, giving the whole team six months of work. Clients told us they chose us because of the strong team and portfolio they found through the site. Our average project value has increased nearly 2×, which was our main goal — to strengthen our brand image. As a digital business card, it&apos;s working perfectly.”
— Client review

A recruitment agency with clients across two continents and no website at all.
ROLE
Website design & build (first Webflow project)
SCOPE
Webflow build, CMS-driven jobs board, brand identity
TOOLS
Figma, Webflow
YEAR
2024
RESULT
Fully self-service site, client-maintained
[Heading2]The Problem

Lead designer
SCOPE
Full site redesign, Figma component system, social & dashboard graphics, sister-brand logo

Figma

2024

[Heading2]The Problem

Built the app&apos;s website solo  —  Designed and built the companion app&apos;s landing page in Wix with a new Figma design system, structured around the app&apos;s actual benefits.
